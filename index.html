Who We Are

EferGames is a web page created as an academic project to represent a modern video game store. The purpose of this page is to show information about the company, its mission, vision, suppliers, products, customers, and contact details. Through the use of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, this project presents an organized, attractive, and interactive design focused on the sale of video games, consoles, and gaming accessories.

About Us
